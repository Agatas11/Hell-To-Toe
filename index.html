What we do

Bring us any pair: sneakers, office shoes, boots or heels.

Heel and sole repair

New heel tips, resoling and glued or stitched soles that hold for years, not weeks.

from 40 zł

Cleaning and polishing

Deep cleaning for sneakers and a proper polish for leather, so your shoes look new again.

from 30 zł

Fit and comfort

Shoe stretching and custom insoles for pairs that pinch, slip or make your feet ache.

from 50 zł

How it works

  1. Book online

    Send us a message with a photo of your shoes and we will reply with a price.

  2. Drop them off

    Bring your shoes to the workshop at a time that suits you.

  3. Walk away happy

    Most repairs are ready in 3 to 5 days. We text you when they are done.

Why Heel To Toe?

  • Repair before replace. We only recommend new shoes when a repair truly isn't worth it.
  • Clear prices. You know the price before we start. No surprises at pick-up.
  • Student discount. 15% off every service with a valid student ID.
